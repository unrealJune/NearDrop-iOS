# LiftDrop Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

LiftDrop transfers files and links directly between nearby devices over the
local network. The developer does not collect, store, sell, or share personal
data from the app.

## Data handling

- LiftDrop does not require an account.
- LiftDrop does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services.
- Files, links, device names, and transfer details are used only to establish
  and complete transfers between devices. They are not sent to the developer
  or uploaded to a developer-operated server.
- Received files remain on your device until you export or delete them.

## Device permissions

LiftDrop uses local-network access to discover and communicate with nearby
devices. It accesses files or content only when you select them for a transfer
or accept an incoming transfer.

## Third parties

LiftDrop interoperates with the Quick Share protocol but is not affiliated
with Google. The developer does not provide personal data to Google or other
third parties through LiftDrop.

## Children

LiftDrop does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when LiftDrop's data practices change. The
effective date above identifies the latest revision.
